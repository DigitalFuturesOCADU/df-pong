# Sound credits

The recorded sounds in this folder come from public archives. Each was trimmed to one moment,
faded, converted to mono MP3 and leveled for DF Pong. Licences were checked on each item's page
when the sounds were added (October 2026).

## Adding sounds

Only add recordings you made yourself, or ones that are public domain, CC0, or CC BY (with a credit here).
Keep them short, mono, and around 100 KB or less, then add a line to `manifest.json`.
